# [C3] Tax: withholding tax, gross-up, tax reporting (CRS / FATCA) — BRIEF

> **Category:** C3 Regulatory, Risk & Compliance · **Difficulty:** ◔ · **Banking-relevant:** yes / 💳

> **One-liner:** Withholding tax = the tax a jurisdiction collects on income generated by non-resident securities. CRS and FATCA = the *reporting* mechanisms that exchange withholding-tax data with home authorities to prevent double taxation and enable enforcement.

> **Why an enterprise architect / trainee cares:** Every dividend, interest, and capital-gain cash-flow must be taxed at source, depleted, and reported. A tax-withholding error is not a missed coupon — it is a regulatory fine, a client dispute, and a cross-border data-privacy problem.

## Quick definition
Withholding tax is the tax levied by the *source* jurisdiction on income generated by securities held by non-residents. The custodian must:
1. Determine the client's tax residency.
2. Apply the correct withholding rate (treaty vs non-treaty).
3. Gross up or net pay as instructed.
4. Report the gross income, tax withheld, and tax paid to the tax authority.

CRS / FATCA are *information-exchange* frameworks: the custodian files a report (CRS returns, FATCA Form 8966) that lets the home country track income and enforce tax.

## Key ideas / terms
- **Withholding tax:** Tax levied at source on non-resident income (dividends, interest, capital gains).
- **Gross-up:** The custodial does NOT net, but the client may request gross payment to avoid a double-tax hole (client reimburses withholding via separate gross-up).
- **CRS:** Common Reporting Standard — 2025/2026 multilateral exchange of tax information among 90+ countries.
- **FATCA:** Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act — US regime requiring US persons' foreign account data.
- **Tax residency:** The country(s) where the beneficial owner is taxable.
- **UBO:** Ultimate Beneficial Owner; the entity or person ultimately controlling the account.
- **TIN:** Taxpayer Identification Number; required for CRS / FATCA reporting.
- **Tax treaty:** Bilateral / multilateral agreement that sets reduced withholding rates / eliminates tax.
- **QF:** Quota funds = one-way consent / bonded account / 2-hr.
- **EIT:** Earned income tax (source-based) vs exit / exit-out (home-based).
- **CDI:** Cross-border data in trust.

## The mental model
Withholding tax is a **4-stage gate**:
1. **Residency determination** — where the client is taxable.
2. **Rate application** — treaty = reduced; non-treaty = full source.
3. **Processing** — gross up or net, as instructed.
4. **Reporting** — CRS / FATCA returns filed to tax authority.

If any gate is manual at volume, the bank is non-compliant.
