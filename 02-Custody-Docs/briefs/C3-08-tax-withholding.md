# [C3] Tax: withholding tax, gross-up, tax reporting (CRS / FATCA) — BRIEF

> **Category:** C3 Regulatory, Risk & Compliance · **Difficulty:** ◔ · **Banking-relevant:** yes / 💳

> **One-liner:** Withholding tax is the tax a source jurisdiction levies on non-resident income (dividends, interest, capital gains). CRS (Common Reporting Standard) and FATCA (Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act) are the information-exchange mechanisms that let the home country track and enforce tax on cross-border income.

> **Why an enterprise architect / trainee cares:** Every dividend, interest, and capital-gain cash-flow must be taxed at source, reported, and transmitted to the client's home tax authority. A withholding-tax error is not a missed coupon — it is a regulatory fine, a client dispute, and a cross-border privacy problem.

## Quick definition
Withholding tax = tax levied at source on income generated by securities held by non-residents. The custodian must determine residency, apply the correct rate (treaty or non-treaty), process gross-up or net payment, and report gross income, tax withheld, and tax paid.

CRS / FATCA are *information-exchange* frameworks: the custodian files a report (CRS returns, FATCA Form 8966) that lets the home country track income and enforce tax.

## Key ideas / terms
- **Withholding tax:** Tax at source on non-resident income (dividends, interest, capital gains).
- **Gross-up:** The custodian DOES NOT net; instead, the client may request gross payment (client reimburses withholding separately).
- **CRS:** Common Reporting Standard — 2025/2026 multilateral exchange of tax information among 90+ countries.
- **FATCA:** Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act — US regime requiring account data for US persons.
- **Tax residency:** Where the beneficial owner is taxable.
- **TIN:** Taxpayer Identification Number; required for CRS / FATCA.
- **UBO:** Ultimate Beneficial Owner; the person or entity ultimately controlling the account.
- **Tax treaty:** Bilateral agreement setting reduced withholding rates or eliminating tax.
- **QF:** Quota funds = one-way consent or bonded account.
- **EIT:** Earned income tax (source-based) vs exit (home-based tax).
- **CDI:** Cross-border data in trust.

## The mental model
Withholding tax is a **4-stage gate**:
1. **Residency determination** (TIN)

2. **Rate application** (treaty = reduced; non-treaty = full source)

3. **Processing** (gross-up or net)

4. **Reporting** (CRS / FATCA transcripts sent to tax authority)

## Banking example
**BNP Paribas** maintains a global withholding-tax engine covering 120+ jurisdictions. A Belgian corporate client receives dividend income from a US source:
- US-source dividend: 15% US withholding under treaty.
- If client lacks treaty eligibility (e.g., non-resident shell), 30% standard rate applies.
- The custodian reports to US IRS (Form W-8BEN / CRS) and Belgian tax authority (DR).

## Common confusions
- **CRS vs FATCA:** CRS = multilateral (90+ countries); FATCA = US-only.
- **Gross-up vs net-pay:** Gross-up = client receives gross, pays withholding separately; net-pay = custodian withholds at source.
- **Treaty vs non-treaty:** Treaty = reduced rate; non-treaty = full source rate.
