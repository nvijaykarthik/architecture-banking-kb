# C3-08 Cloud Platform — BRIEF
> **Category:** C3 — Architecture Domains · **Difficulty:** ◑ · **Banking-relevant:** yes
> **One-liner:** Selecting, governing, and leveraging cloud subscription capabilities—so that the 💳 bank's multi-region data, real-time payments, and digital channels run with regulatory compliance, resilient locality, and cost control.
> **Why an EA cares:** Cloud is not a monolith; choosing the wrong provider or region writes data-sovereignty, latency, and DORA obligations into stone for the next decade—often with costly migration later.

## Quick definition
Cloud platform architecture is the design and governance of multi-cloud or single-cloud environments that host banking workloads—ensuring compliance, resilience, observability, and cost control.
